Notes for the build (v1.5). (1) Statement tabs sit beside Key financials and share its Quarterly / Annual toggle: five quarters side by side, no change columns (comparisons stay on Key financials). Bold rows are totals; indented rows sit inside the total above; lines needn't add up to totals and nothing is plugged. Operating expenses are shown line by line as the company presents them: its own lines, labels and order, read from each filing's income statement, including lines on the company's own tags (Amazon's Fulfillment and Technology and infrastructure). Totals keep standard names, since captions change between filings ("Net sales" vs "Total net sales"). A line filed only in the 10-K has no derivable Q4 and shows MISSING; that column's lines then won't add to its total. A line whose company tag changes between filings is joined only when both tags report the same value in a shared period. Two derived rows only: after-tax items (hidden when zero) and net new debt. Statement lines are extracted once per filing and stored; the tab loads after the verdict, so it never slows the board. (2) Two new findings, BORROWING and ACQUISITIONS AND INVESTMENTS, after heavy investment; a third, RETURNS (buybacks plus dividends over four quarters above free cash flow over the same quarters, and above zero), doesn't fire on AMZN. Asset sales dropped. All three are display only: no effect on risk and not used in the Summary. (3) Signs follow the money on every statement: inflows and income positive; costs, tax and outflows negative (capex, acquisitions, investment purchases, debt repaid, buybacks, dividends), so columns add down. XBRL files payments as positive amounts; the sign is applied for display only, never to the stored value. Red is kept for figures negative against their nature (a loss, negative free cash flow, a non-operating loss); net new debt below zero means net repayment and stays plain; ordinary costs and payments show the minus in plain type, so red still means something. (4) The fiscal year sits next to the company name and in the board's identity line, taken from EDGAR's fiscal year end and the filer's own 10-K period ends, so FY26 or Q4 is never read against the calendar year. (5) Each statement figure carries its change on the previous quarter (or fiscal year) in small type beneath it, using the Key financials rules: % only when both figures are positive, otherwise the change in dollars, never coloured; none for the oldest column or when either figure is missing. (6) What stands out gets filter chips (All, P&L, Balance sheet, Cash flow, each with its count) and every finding carries small tags for the statements it reads, which open that tab. A finding reading two statements shows under both (payables: balance sheet and P&L; borrowing: cash flow and balance sheet). Terms and any red flag stay visible under every filter: a filter must never hide a reason to escalate. Red flags are tagged Filings, cash burn Cash flow and Balance sheet, spending cuts P&L or Filings, margin and losses P&L, returns Cash flow. (7) No separate "From the filing" box: Claude's explanation is the finding's own closing sentence, followed by a small citation (form and section; hover or tap for filing date, section and accession, with the passages relied on). A sentence that fails the verbatim check is dropped, and so is the finding's explanation when the filing doesn't give one: the finding then ends at its rule-based sentence. With Claude off, every finding still reads complete. (8) One-off figures line shows pre-tax income; payables days carry one decimal so the change matches the figures shown.
