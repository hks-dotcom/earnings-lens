Notes for the build. (1) A strip under the six tiles, not a seventh tile: two bars per period (latest quarter and the same quarter last year) on one shared scale, the net figure in the header, the components beneath. Display only: no effect on the ladder, the Summary or the findings. (2) Cash and short-term investments = the runway's liquidity, same rule: short-term investments never filed → cash only, labelled "Cash"; filed but missing for the quarter → cash only, and the line says so. (3) Debt = long-term debt including the part due within a year + short-term borrowings (the statement tab's rows). Leases excluded, and the hover says so. If either debt row is missing for the quarter, debt and net show MISSING. A company that has never tagged debt shows "No debt tagged" and no net figure. (4) Net reads "Net Debt $X" when debt is larger, "Net Cash $X" when liquidity is larger (capital N and D/C, everywhere including the Copy brief). Neutral colours only: teal for cash, slate for debt; nothing red or green. (5) Amounts in $B with one decimal (one decimal in $M under $100M quarterly revenue, as elsewhere). The Copy brief gains one line: "Liquidity vs debt: $123.0B vs $133.3B, Net Debt $10.3B (Net Cash $36.9B a year ago)." (6) Every tile has two parts: the metric, and one benchmark line. The formula lives only in the (i) hover. Benchmarks are reference points, not rules, declared in the footer: current ratio above 1.0x; debt/equity below 1.0; DSO within 45 days; DPO within 45 days (the terms ceiling, so a company inside it pays suppliers within the longest terms we'd offer); Altman Z'' above 2.6 (the rules' own safe zone). Red flags show the count only, with no benchmark line. The footer adds: "Benchmarks vary by industry: retailers often run a current ratio below 1.0x by design." No colour, no tick or cross against the benchmark. Current ratio, debt/equity and DSO values here are computed from the filed figures for the mock; on the page every value is the app's own. (7) Phone: tiles in two columns, the two periods stacked. (8) The bars keep one shared scale, without saying so on the page. The operating-segments line under the tiles stays exactly as live.
